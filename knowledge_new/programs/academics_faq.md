#### What is the minimum attendance percentage required to be eligible to write final exams?

Students must maintain a minimum of 75% attendance in each registered course to be eligible to sit for the final semester examinations.

#### What happens if my attendance falls below the minimum required percentage?

If your attendance in a course falls below 75% without an approved medical or official excuse, you will be barred from taking the final exam for that course and will receive an 'F' (Fail due to lack of attendance) grade.

#### How do I request an attendance waiver or medical leave of absence?

You must submit a formal leave application along with a valid medical certificate from a registered physician to your Head of Department within three working days of returning to campus.

#### Are attendance records updated in real-time, and where can I view them?

Attendance is updated weekly by course instructors. You can view your current attendance percentage for each course on the student portal dashboard under 'Academic Records'.

#### Do university-sponsored sports, events, or field trips count as excused absences?

Yes, representing the university in sports, cultural events, or academic conferences is counted as 'Duty Leave'. You must submit an authorization letter from the activities coordinator to clear your absence.

#### Where can I find the final exam schedule and my seating arrangement?

The exam timetable is published on the university website and student portal 30 days before exams begin. Room assignments and seating details are posted on the portal 3 days prior to the start of examinations.

#### What is the policy for rescheduling an exam in case of a direct timetable conflict?

If you have two exams scheduled at the same time, you must report this conflict to the Office of the Controller of Examinations at least 14 days before exams start to arrange a makeup slot.

#### What happens if I miss a final exam due to a medical emergency or unforeseen event?

You must apply for a makeup exam by submitting a medical certificate or emergency proof to the Dean of Academic Affairs within 48 hours of the missed exam. If approved, you will be allowed to sit for a makeup exam scheduled during the next break.

#### Are students allowed to use calculators or reference materials during examinations?

Only non-programmable calculators are permitted in exam halls unless the course syllabus or exam paper explicitly specifies that scientific calculators or open-book reference materials are allowed.

#### What actions constitute academic malpractice during an exam, and what are the penalties?

Malpractice includes carrying unauthorized notes, using smart devices, copying, or communicating during an exam. Penalties range from a score of zero in the exam to suspension for an entire semester or expulsion.

#### How do I apply for paper revaluation or re-totalling of my exam marks?

You can apply online through the student portal by selecting 'Revaluation Request', selecting the specific course, and submitting the digital application form.

#### What is the deadline to submit a request for revaluation after the results are declared?

The revaluation application must be submitted within 7 working days from the date of the online publication of the semester results. Late submissions are not accepted under any circumstances.

#### Is there a fee associated with applying for paper revaluation, and is it refundable?

Yes, there is a fee of $35 per course for revaluation. If the revaluation results in an upward grade change of one full letter grade or higher, the fee will be fully refunded to your student account.

#### Can my grades decrease after a revaluation process, or do they only increase?

Revaluation involves a complete re-grading of the paper. Your grade can increase, decrease, or remain unchanged. The score obtained during the revaluation process will be finalized as your final grade.

#### How long does the revaluation process take, and where will the updated grades be published?

The revaluation process typically takes 15 to 20 working days. The revised marks and grades will be updated directly on your student portal and in your official academic record.

#### What is a course credit, and how many credits do I need to graduate from a degree program?

A credit represents the number of contact hours per week. Generally, a 4-year undergraduate degree requires successful completion of at least 120 to 130 credits, depending on the major.

#### What is the maximum number of credits I can register for in a single semester?

The standard semester load is 15-18 credits. With special permission from your academic advisor, students with a CGPA above 3.5 may overload up to a maximum of 21 credits.

#### What is the difference between core credits, elective credits, and audit courses?

Core credits are mandatory courses required for your major. Elective credits allow you to choose topics of interest. Audit courses are taken for knowledge without earning credit or impacting your GPA.

#### Can I transfer credits earned from another university or an online platform?

Yes, credits from accredited institutions can be transferred if the course syllabus matches our curriculum by at least 80%. Online credits (MOOCs) are eligible if pre-approved by the department head.

#### What is the process for dropping a course, and does it impact my registered credits?

You can drop a course during the designated 'Add/Drop' period (typically the first two weeks of class) online. Dropping after this period results in a 'W' (Withdrawn) on your transcript and does not refund the credits.

#### How is CGPA calculated, and what is the difference between SGPA and CGPA?

SGPA (Semester Grade Point Average) is the GPA for a single semester. CGPA (Cumulative Grade Point Average) is the average of all SGPAs weighted by the credit hours of the courses taken across all semesters.

#### What is the minimum CGPA required to remain in good academic standing and avoid academic probation?

Students must maintain a minimum CGPA of 2.0. If your CGPA falls below 2.0, you will be placed on academic probation for the following semester to improve your standing.

#### How does repeating a course to improve my grade affect my CGPA calculation?

When you repeat a course, the new grade replaces the older grade in the CGPA calculation, although both attempts will remain listed on your transcript.

#### Is a failing grade (like 'F') included in the CGPA calculation, and what happens when I clear the course?

Yes, an 'F' grade carries 0 grade points and is included in the CGPA calculation until you retake and pass the course, at which point the passing grade replaces the 'F' in your CGPA calculation.

#### How do I request an official academic transcript containing my semester-wise GPA and CGPA?

You can request an official transcript through the 'Academic Records' section on the student portal or by visiting the Registrar's Office. Standard physical copies take 3-5 business days.

#### When is the academic timetable released for the upcoming semester?

The academic timetable, including class slots and sections, is published on the portal 14 days before the registration period starts.

#### Can I request a change in my class section or timetable slot?

Class sections can only be changed during the Add/Drop week, subject to seat availability in the desired section. Timetable slots for individual sections are fixed and cannot be changed.

#### Where do I find details about my lecture rooms, labs, and professor office hours?

Room and laboratory locations are printed on your registered course schedule in the portal. Professor office hours and contact locations are listed in the syllabus of each course.

#### How do holiday schedules or weather closures affect the regular timetable?

Makeup classes for missed days due to holidays or weather closures are usually scheduled on subsequent Saturdays, and details are announced by the registrar via email and portal notifications.

#### What should I do if two of my registered courses have conflicting lecture timings in the timetable?

The enrollment portal prevents registration for conflicting classes. If a conflict arises due to a rescheduling, you must contact your academic advisor to change one of the courses.
