#### What grading system does PES University use for the final semester examinations?

PES University uses an absolute grading system based on marks obtained. The grade scale is:

| Grade | Marks Range | Grade Points |
|-------|------------|--------------|
| O (Outstanding) | 90–100 | 10 |
| A+ (Excellent) | 80–89 | 9 |
| A (Very Good) | 70–79 | 8 |
| B+ (Good) | 60–69 | 7 |
| B (Above Average) | 55–59 | 6 |
| C (Average) | 50–54 | 5 |
| P (Pass) | 40–49 | 4 |
| F (Fail) | Below 40 | 0 |

#### What is the passing marks requirement for each subject?

Students must score a minimum of 40% marks (both in the internal assessments and the end-semester examination) to pass a course. Scoring below 40% in either component results in an 'F' (Fail) grade.

#### How is the internal assessment component structured?

The internal assessment (IA) typically constitutes 50% of the total marks for most courses. This includes mid-semester tests, assignments, lab work, and continuous evaluation components. The remaining 50% comes from the end-semester examination.

#### What is the minimum SGPA required to be eligible for the Prof. MRD Scholarship?

To be eligible for the Prof. MRD Scholarship, a student must be in the Top 5% of their class based on their CGPA at the end of the academic year. There is no fixed minimum SGPA figure — it depends on the overall class performance.

#### What is the minimum SGPA required for the Prof. CNR Rao Scholarship?

To be eligible for the Prof. CNR Rao Scholarship, a student must fall within the next 20% of their class (i.e., ranked between 5% and 25%) based on their SGPA for the semester. The scholarship provides a Merit Certificate and a 40% Tuition Fee Refund.

#### Are scholarship eligibility criteria evaluated every year?

Yes. The scholarship eligibility conditions, including the qualifying CGPA/SGPA thresholds and the top percentage criteria, are stipulated by the Scholarship Committee each year and are subject to change. Scholarships are not applicable to students in their final year of study. A student is eligible for only one award per semester or academic year.

#### When are the course work examinations scheduled for PhD students?

Course work examinations for PhD and M.Tech (Research) students are scheduled in February/March and August of each year. The PhD and M.Tech Research entrance examination is held on 14 March 2026. For exact dates, please check the official academic calendar or contact the Exam Cell at exam.cell@pes.edu.

#### What is the grading pattern for PhD course work?

PhD course work is evaluated through a combination of continuous internal assessments and an end-semester examination. Students must obtain a minimum grade of 'B' (equivalent to 55% marks) in all course work subjects to proceed to the comprehensive examination and thesis stage.

#### What happens if a PhD student fails their course work examination?

If a PhD scholar fails to obtain the minimum required grade in any course work subject, they are given one additional chance to appear for the supplementary examination in the next scheduled examination cycle (February/March or August). Repeated failure may lead to de-registration from the program.

#### How do I check my semester results and grades at PES University?

Semester results are published on the official PES University student portal (ERP system). You will receive a notification via your registered university email once results are declared. Detailed subject-wise marks and grades are available under the 'Academic Records' section of the portal.

#### Is there a grade improvement option for passed courses (re-examination for grade improvement)?

No, PES University does not offer grade improvement re-examinations for courses already passed. However, students who score below the minimum 40% threshold can reappear for those failed courses in the next available examination cycle as a supplementary (back) examination.

#### What is the Distinction Award and how is it given?

The Distinction Award at PES University is a Merit Certificate awarded to students who secure an SGPA of 7.75 or above in a semester. This is separate from the MRD and CNR Rao scholarships and does not carry a fee refund component.
