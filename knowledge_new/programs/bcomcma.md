Introducing the B.Com (Hons) with CMA program at PES University, Bangalore – a dynamic blend of academic excellence and professional expertise. This innovative undergraduate course is designed to equip students with a comprehensive understanding of commerce while offering a specialized focus on Certified Management Accountancy (CMA). Notably, this program does not have an exit option.

Our B.Com integrated with CMA program seamlessly merges the foundational aspects of a Bachelor of Commerce (B.Com) with the strategic insights of Certified Management Accountant (CMA) certification, provided by the Institute of Management Accountants (IMA), USA. The course is globally recognized in 150 countries, with over 100,000 certifications earned worldwide. It’s an advanced-level credential appropriate for accountants and financial professionals in business.

The B.Com (Hons) program will consist of 160 credits spread over four years and eight semesters. Students will be interning in the corporate sector for two semesters, in the third and fourth years, respectively. Students will secure a B.Com (Hons) degree with a Strategic Finance specialization after the successful completion of all subjects.

Our dedicated faculty, industry-aligned curriculum, and state-of-the-art facilities create an enriching learning environment for aspiring professionals. Graduates of this program will emerge with a strong foundation in commerce, coupled with the prestigious CMA qualification, positioning them as sought-after professionals in the world of finance and accounting.

Prepare for a successful career in commerce with the B.Com (Hons) with CMA program at PES University – where academic excellence meets professional acumen.

### Curriculum Structure

CMA Part 1: Financial Planning, Performance, and Analytics

This part focuses on foundational elements of financial management, providing students with essential skills in the following areas:

1. External Financial Reporting Decisions:Emphasizes financial reporting standards, preparing financial statements, and analysing external reports.
2. Cost Management:Focuses on budgeting, cost control, and effective management of operational costs.
3. Internal Controls:Covers risk management, internal auditing practices, and ensuring regulatory compliance.
4. Planning, Budgeting, and Forecasting:Teaches strategic financial planning, revenue forecasting, and cash flow management.
5. Technology and Analytics:Involves the use of financial data analytics and modern technological tools for decision-making.
6. Performance Management:Helps in evaluating organizational performance through KPIs (Key Performance Indicators) and strategic tools.

CMA Part 2: Strategic Financial Management

This part advances the financial expertise of students, focusing on strategic decision-making and financial management.

1. Financial Statement Analysis:Develops skills to analyze and interpret financial statements for decision-making.
2. Corporate Finance:Focuses on managing a company’s financial resources, including capital structuring and financing decisions.
3. Risk Management:Teaches techniques to identify, assess, and mitigate financial risks in business operations.
4. Investment Decisions:Guides in making informed investment choices and managing portfolios.
5. Decision Analysis:Involves strategic decision-making based on financial analysis and forecasting models.
6. Professional Ethics:Emphasizes ethical practices and standards in financial management and decision-making.

### Pedagogy

- Interactive Lectures: Engage students through interactive lectures that blend theoretical concepts with real-world examples. Encourage active participation, questions, and discussions to enhance understanding.
- Case Studies: Integrate case studies derived from actual business scenarios to apply theoretical knowledge to practical situations. This hands-on approach fosters critical thinking and problem-solving skills.
- Guest Lectures and Industry Insights: Invite industry experts and professionals to deliver guest lectures, providing students with valuable insights into current industry practices, trends, and challenges.
- Workshops and Seminars: Conduct workshops and seminars focused on CMA exam preparation, practical accounting software usage, and soft skills development. These sessions bridge the gap between academia and industry expectations.
- Professional Certification Support: Offer dedicated support for CMA exam preparation, including study groups, practice exams, and mentoring. This ensures that students are well-prepared for certification, enhancing their employability.
- Internships and Practical Experience: Facilitate internships and practical experiences with industry partners, allowing students to apply classroom learning to real-world situations, thus enhancing their practical skills and professional acumen.
- Technology Integration: Leverage modern teaching technologies, simulations, and accounting software to familiarize students with the tools and technologies used in the industry, preparing them for a tech-savvy work environment.
- Research Projects: Encourage students to undertake research projects related to emerging trends in commerce and management accounting. This promotes independent thinking and a deeper understanding of evolving industry dynamics.
- Continuous Assessment: Implement a system of continuous assessment through quizzes, assignments, and presentations, providing regular feedback to students and promoting a consistent and thorough understanding of the subjects.

### Career Prospects

Following are some of the future career prospects for the graduates of our B.Com (Hons) with CMA program:

- Certified Management Accountant (CMA): Graduates will be equipped with the prestigious CMA credential, enhancing their global recognition and employability in management accounting and financial management roles.
- Financial Analysts: Prepare for roles as financial analysts, providing expertise in budgeting, forecasting, and financial planning to guide strategic decision-making within organizations.
- Management Accountants: Assume responsibilities as management accountants, contributing to the development of financial strategies, cost management, and performance evaluation.
- Corporate Finance Professionals: Pursue careers in corporate finance, working on capital budgeting, mergers and acquisitions, and financial risk management for organizations.
- Auditors: Explore opportunities as auditors, ensuring financial compliance and accuracy in financial reporting for businesses and regulatory bodies.
- Tax Consultants: Specialize in taxation and become tax consultants, advising businesses on tax planning, compliance, and strategies to optimize tax liabilities.
- Business Consultants: Serve as business consultants, leveraging financial acumen to provide advisory services for improving organizational efficiency and profitability.
- Entrepreneurial Ventures: Launch and manage entrepreneurial ventures, applying financial expertise to ensure the success and sustainability of new business initiatives.
- Financial Planners: Excel as financial planners, guiding individuals and businesses in managing investments, retirement planning, and wealth accumulation.
- Global Opportunities: The CMA qualification opens doors to global opportunities, allowing graduates to pursue careers internationally, work with multinational corporations, or engage in cross-border financial management.

### Placement Opportunities & Career Assistance

Placements have always been the USP of PES University. Professional placement assistance will be provided through the centralised placement cell of the university, along with organised training in soft skills, aptitude, etiquette, resume building, etc., from the first semester itself to instil the necessary qualities for students to succeed in campus placements.

In addition, the following will be extended to all our B.Com (Hons) with CMA students:

- Leverage of our Alumni Network: Graduates benefit from a strong and active alumni network that provides mentorship, networking opportunities, and insights into various career paths. Alumni often play a pivotal role in connecting current students with employment opportunities.
- Internship Opportunities: The university facilitates internships with leading organisations, allowing students to gain practical experience, apply classroom knowledge, and establish connections within the industry.
- Career Counselling: The university offers personalised career counselling services to help students identify their strengths, interests, and career goals. This guidance assists students in making informed decisions about their professional journeys.
