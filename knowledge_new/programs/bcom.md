The Bachelor of commerce programme is designed to equip the students with accounting and
                            financial skills and build competencies required to succeed in the field of commerce. The
                            three/four year programme will equip you with knowledge of accounting, principles, export and
                            import laws, economic policies and other aspects that influence trade and business. B. Com is a
                            renowned undergraduate course with many job opportunities. It lays a strong foundation for
                            prospective learners. The B.Com degree course aims to teach students analytical abilities,
                            financial management, and business skills.

Students who wish to pursue only BCom, will have classes for four hours a day.
