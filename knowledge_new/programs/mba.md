### Overview of MBA @ PESU

The **Master of Business Administration (MBA)** program at PES University is a two-year, globally recognised course designed to cultivate leadership, analytical, and managerial skills. With its rigorous curriculum, the MBA program integrates academic learning and real-world exposure, empowering graduates to excel in diverse industries. Ranked as the **#1 University in Karnataka and 24th among India’s top management institutes** (Times of India B-School Rankings), PES University offers unparalleled academic and professional development opportunities.

### The PES University Edge

The PES University MBA offers one of the most transformative MBA experiences. Ranked as the #1 University in Karnataka, PES University is committed to excellence in education. The program is designed to support professional and personal growth through an immersive and holistic learning experience. Balancing academic rigor with an experiential approach, our curriculum provides the knowledge and tools to succeed in a rapidly changing world. At PES, we teach the use of analytical frameworks and apply evidence-based thinking to solve complex problems and make a real impact in business and industry. Learning continues well beyond the classroom through overseas student exchanges, international study trips, case competitions, internships, and more.

“Your PES MBA Journey: Redefining Business with Research and Strategy!”

The MBA curriculum at PESU, with its case studies, innovative teaching methods, exposure to industry best practices, and experiential learning, emphasizes the distinctive challenges of managing real-world issues in a global context. Various clubs, social projects, action-oriented courses, contemporary studies, and student self-governance initiatives help develop critical thinking and leadership skills.

### Program Differentiators (PESU X Factors)

- Research Paper: Students are encouraged to write impactful research papers, fostering analytical thinking and research aptitude.
- Case Studies: Real-world problem-solving through global business case studies and collaborative industry projects.
- Peer-to-Peer Learning: Collaborative learning through Student Peer Groups and Peer Evaluations enhancing mutual knowledge-sharing and team dynamics.
- Exposure: Hackathons, Studyathons, Readathons, CXO Talks, and Leadership Summits, International Academic Partnerships, Comprehensive training in tools like Tableau, Python, R, and Advanced Analytics, with certifications in emerging technologies.
- Mentoring: Personalised guidance from faculty and industry professionals to align academic and career goals.

### Pedagogy

- **Case-Based Learning:** Integrate case studies drawn from real business scenarios to connect theoretical concepts with practical application. This method develops analytical thinking, problem-solving skills, and decision-making under uncertainty.
- **Research and Presentations:** Encourage students to write research papers and deliver presentations on contemporary issues, enhancing research aptitude, critical thinking, public speaking, and emotional resilience.
- **Hackathons, Studyathons, and Readathons:** Promote collaborative and competitive learning environments through intensive events that nurture innovation, deep analysis, and reading beyond the curriculum.
- **Workshops and Certificate Programs:** Conduct workshops and specialised certificate courses on leadership, strategy, and emerging technologies to build industry-relevant skills and keep students aligned with current trends.
- **Interactive Class Participation:** Foster meaningful classroom engagement by encouraging respectful expression of ideas, critical dialogue, and peer learning, thereby enriching the academic experience.
- **Networking with CXOs:** Facilitate student interaction with over 100+ CXOs through talks and networking events, offering real-world insights, leadership exposure, and valuable professional connections. ### Curriculum

The MBA course at PES University consists of a core curriculum, an internship, and elective courses over a two-year period, during which participants fuel their personal and professional growth through a combination of classroom and experiential learning opportunities. The dynamic and diverse environment, with case-based teaching, a focus on research article writing, and presentations, encourages each student to prepare on topics of contemporary relevance.

### Specializations

##### PES University MBA offers major in the following areas…

**Business Analytics** Specialization will prepare students in Data Analytical skills for which there is intense demand, taught through SAS, Tableau and R.

**Finance** Specialization is rooted in building expertise in the burgeoning financial and banking services.

**Hospital & Healthcare Management** Specialization will enable the students to handle the business side intricacies in the hospital & healthcare industry with utmost professionalism and expertise.

**Marketing** Will provide a strong base in digital marketing and e-commerce.

**Human Resource** Human Resource in the digital era trains students to manage and harness human talent in the knowledge economy.

**Operations Management** Will enable the students to manage a business through design, planning, control, coordination and improvement of business processes, systems and resources essential to meet consumers’ needs.

### Centers of Excellence(COE)

CoE’s have been set up under the management department at PES University in the areas of Digital Marketing, Leadership, Capital Markets, Entrepreneurship, Teaching & Learning and more to study the most recent developments in the industry and to help the industry by solving real-world problems by carrying out live industry projects.

### Leadership & Team Building Activities

Regular outbound team building activities will be conducted by the management department at PES University to train our students in the vital area of teamwork, camaraderie, coordination and execution.

### Program Focus

The MBA curriculum at PES University is meticulously designed to blend theoretical knowledge with practical applications:
