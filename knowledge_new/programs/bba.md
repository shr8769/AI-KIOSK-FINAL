Bachelor of Business Administration: BBA 3
                            year and BBA (Hons) 4-year Program is one of
                            the best undergraduate programs available nationally. BBA is a discipline of business
                            studies that teaches us how to do business, comprehend people, manage people, handle
                            difficult and critical client interactions, and communicate effectively.

Our students graduate with the ability to
                            adapt to an intellectually and technologically
                            changing environment. Over the years, we have accomplished this with the participative
                            efforts of the university, staff, students and parents. PES University is the second
                            most preferred destination next to IIMB for B-School aspirants (Times of India).

## Program Differentiators(PESU X Factors)

Research Papers:Encourages students to author impactful research papers,
                            blending academic rigour with industry relevance.

Case Studies:Real-world business problem analysis with practical
                            solutions, Peer to Peer learning.

Exposure:Conclaves and Summits, Guest lectures, B-Talks and E-Talks,
                            Hackathons, Studyathons, Tech competitions, Alumni Interactions, Curricular and
                            Co-Curricular Forums and Committees, Industry Visits.

Certification:Certifications in Python, R, Tableau, Machine Learning, AI,
                            JMP, Tableau, Excel and PowerBI, Project Management, Design Thinking.

Mentoring:2 one-on-one mentoring per semester.

Graduating Outcomes:Entrepreneurship Development Activities, Placement
                            Training, Higher Education Workshop.
