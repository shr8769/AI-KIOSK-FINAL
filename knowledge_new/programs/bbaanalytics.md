BBA in Business Analytics is an undergraduate four-year degree program that combines the fields of Business Management and Data Analysis. It equips students with the skills necessary to analyze large datasets, identify patterns and trends, and use that information to make informed business decisions. Our students graduate with the ability to adapt to an intellectually and technologically changing environment. Over the years, we have accomplished this with the participative efforts of the University, staff, students and parents. PES University is the second most preferred destination next to IIMB for B-School aspirants (Times of India). PES University is ranked fifth in the Outlook-ICARE India University Rankings 2020 and is the only University in Karnataka to rank in the top 30.

BBA Business Analytics graduates enjoy excellent career prospects both in India and abroad. This field has revolutionized the global business landscape, with most organizations now integrating business analytics into their daily operations. As a result, there is a growing demand for BBA Business Analytics graduates in the international market. Numerous national, multinational, and e-commerce companies are actively hiring these professionals, offering attractive salary packages and additional job benefits. Equipped with strong analytical skills, graduates are well-prepared to enter a wide range of industries that rely on data-driven decision-making, including finance, marketing, healthcare, and technology.

### Curriculum Design

Our students graduate with the ability to adapt to an intellectual and technologically changing environment. The BBA (Business Analytics) program at PES University provides students with a comprehensive education aimed at preparing them for the challenges of the business world. The Program Curriculum is well mapped with National & International colleges and universities. Based on the current trends it is updated as per the corporate requirements.

Throughout the program, students delve into core subjects such as Mathematics for Data Sciences, Data and Decisions, Operations Research Project Management, establishing a strong foundational knowledge base. From the 3rd semester onwards, elective courses like Tableau, Python, Machine Learning, AI, SQL, Big Data, Marketing analytics, Financial Analytics, International Business, Supply chain analytics, and HR Analytics allow students to tailor their studies to their career interests in the area of Business Management. The curriculum is well planned in a sequence of instructions consisting of Units meant for developing employability and Skills competencies of students opting for Skills essential for overall development

Practical learning is emphasized, with students gaining hands-on experience using software tools like JMP, Python, Tableau, Excel and Power BI for data analysis. Innovative teaching methods, including flipped classes and real-life case studies, enhance problem-solving and critical thinking skills.

### Assessments

The department employs a continuous evaluation system to support students’ learning journey. 50% of the marks are derived from internal assessments, while the remaining 50% are from end-semester evaluations. Students undergo two internal assessments each semester, along with other components such as research projects, case studies, and worksheets. This diverse approach ensures students’ comprehension of course material and their ability to apply concepts to real-world scenarios. The system encourages active engagement and gives students regular feedback on their progress. The department ensures comprehensive evaluation by combining internal and end-semester assessments while promoting continuous learning and growth among students.
