##### Institute of Medicine

#### Bachelor of Medicine, Bachelor of Surgery (MBBS)

### Method of Admission

Admissions are possible through the following quota’s…

- Government (G) quota
- Private (P) quota
- NRI (N) quota
- Others (Q) quota

Admission for all the seats of all the above four quota’s are through the KEA (Karnataka Examination Authority) counseling process only based on the NEET rank / score of the candidate.

The steps involved in the admissions process at our University after securing the admission through the KEA process as mentioned above is given below. Please note, the below steps are relevant only for the candidates who posses the admission order from KEA after attending the counseling process and not for any other candidates.

**Step 1:** Registration

Registration at PESUIMSR within the duration as specified by Karnataka Examination Authority (KEA)

Candidate’s Mobile Number & Email ID is mandatory for registration.

**Step 2:** Verification and Collection of Documents

Original documents to be submitted at KEA / Institution will be strictly in accordance with the Rules & Regulations set by the Karnataka Examination Authority (ΚΕΑ)

Before reporting to the University, candidates are advised to arrange the documents in the exact order as per the link  [https://pes.edu/admissions/documents-required/](https://pes.edu/admissions/documents-required/) .

**Step 3:** Filling of Application Form at PESUIMSR

After step 2, candidates will be required to fill an application form in the in-house ERP system at PESUIMSR providing comprehensive details.

Candidates should compulsorily carry their recent scanned copy of the passport size photograph (not older than 2 months), preferably taken with a white background.

**Step 4:** Signature on the Application & Declaration Forms

Candidates and Parent / Guardian shall sign the Application Form along with all the declaration forms.

**Step 5:** Payment of Fees and Collection of Fee Receipt

Apart from the fee payment made at the KEA premises, payment of the University miscellaneous fee is compulsory at the time of admission to the University as per the reporting date specified by KEA.

Miscellaneous fee shall be paid through a Demand draft (DD) in favour of “PESUIMSR”, payable at Bangalore.

Mode of Payment will be through Demand Draft (DD) only. CASH / CHEQUE / UPI / Card Payments will not be accepted.

After payment of the miscellaneous fee, candidates should collect the miscellaneous fee paid receipt from the University without fail.

The candidates who have paid less than the approved tuition fee for the first year at KEA, will have to pay the complete tuition fee at the University

**Step 6:** Final Verification

Signed Application form

Signed Declaration forms

Admission copy of fee paid receipt

Document submission Acknowledgement (Office copy)

**Note:**

All admissions are subject to fulfilment of the eligibility criteria by the candidate.

If it is found at a later stage that the candidate has given false information / certificate or is found to have concealed some information, his / her admission will be cancelled without any prior notice. It is the responsibility of the candidates to ensure that they fulfil all the eligibility criteria for the course applied.

Please note that the official website of PESUIMSR is www.pes.edu. There are no other official websites except this.

##### Institute of Architecture

#### Bachelor of Architecture (B.Arch)

1. No candidate shall be admitted to architecture course unless she/he has passed 10+2 or equivalent examination with Physics and Mathematics as compulsory subjects along with either Chemistry or Biology or Technical Vocational subject or Computer Science or Information Technology or Informatics Practices or Engineering Graphics or Business Studies with at least 45% marks in aggregate or passed 10+3 Diploma Examination with Mathematics as compulsory subject with atleast 45% marks in aggregate.

3. Minimum NATA score (eligibility) required for

- KCET seat – Depends on KCET counseling process

**Mode of Admissions**

Karnataka CET – Candidate to appear for KCET counseling based on the NATA score and 2nd PUC/ 12th std scores to secure admission.

PESU Merit Quota – Candidate to register for B.Arch from  [https://admissions.pes.edu/](https://admissions.pes.edu/)  and appear for the counseling at PES University conducted based on the NATA score and 12th std/ 2nd PUC scores to secure admission.

Higher Fee Category (Management Quota) – Seat can be pre-booked. Please contact the Office of Admissions at PES University for further information on this or write to admissions@pes.edu.

##### **General Admission Norms**

- Candidates who have done their education through distance learning mode / online mode / part-time mode / private schooling / IGNOU / open schooling (except for NIOS) / NTTF / NWAC board / IHM / University not recognized by UGC, are not eligible for admission to any program
- Candidates from IGCSE / Cambridge board must have studied atleast 4 subjects in AS level and 4 subjects in A level. Anything less than this will not be acceptable for admission to any of our programs
- Candidates from International Baccalaureate (IB) board

- Original 12th Diploma Transcript of grades is mandatory for admissions
- Must have atleast 3 HL subjects and not more than 3 SL subjects out of their 6 total subjects in the 12th std equivalent original diploma transcript
- Must have secured a minimum of 24 points in total in their 12th std equivalent diploma transcript
- The original 12th equivalent diploma transcript result should clearly mention as “Diploma Awarded”
- Candidates from CBSE board

- If it says “ER (Essential Repeat)” in the 12th certificate, such candidates will not be eligible for admission to any program
- If it says “Compartment” next to results in the 12th marks card, candidates can only do admission after clearing the compartment subject and getting the result as Pass.
- Candidates from Andhra Pradesh board / Telangana board / Kerala board

- Only the 2nd inter / 12th marks will be considered for ascertaining eligibility and for admissions.
