### Loyalty Program

At PES Group of Institutions, we deeply value the trust and dedication of our employees, students, alumni, and their families. As a token of our appreciation, we are proud to introduce the PES Loyalty Program, designed to provide benefits to those who have been a part of the PES family.

We are pleased to announce the extension of our Loyalty Benefits and Scholarship Policy for our students and families. As a token of appreciation forthe continued trust and association with the institution, the following benefits will be applicable from the academic year 2026-27:

###### Grade 11/12 Continuity:

- Students who have studied at Amaatra in Grades 9 and 10 and continue their education atAmaatra for Grades 11 and 12 will be eligible for a 20% fee waiver.
- If students from our sister institutions (PES Public School, SSM School) wish to seekadmission for grades 11 and 12 in The Amaatra Academy, they would be eligible for 10% feewaiver.

###### Grade 11/12 Scholarship:

- The top 5% of students in Grade 11/12 will be awarded 50% of tuition fee as scholarship atthe end of the academic year.
- The next 20% of students will be awarded 40% of tuition fees as scholarship at the end of theacademic year.

###### Extended Loyalty Benefit:

PES Management Quota:

- Students who complete their education at Amaatra from Grades 9 through 12 will be eligiblefor a 20% fee waiver in any non-medical course under PES Management Quota.
- If a student has studied only grade 11 & 12 in Amaatra, then he/she will be eligible for 10%fee waiver in any non-medical course.

###### Sibling Loyalty Benefit:

A 10% waiver on tuition fees will be extended to siblings studying at Amaatra. (Note: If the sibling withdraws before graduating from the 12th, the waiver will be withdrawn too.)

###### PES/Amaatra Staff Benefit:

Children of PES/Amaatra staff will receive a 30% fee waiver in all non-medical management quota seats in any PES Institution.

###### PES Alumni Benefit:

Children of PES alumni will be eligible for a 10% waiver on tuition fees.

With this initiative, PES Group reaffirms its commitment to fostering academic excellence and lifelongconnections. We take immense pride in our PES community and we strive to make quality education more accessible for the Amaatra and PES families.

The management reserves the right to either modify or terminate the program without prior notice.

For further clarification, parents may contact the school administration office.
