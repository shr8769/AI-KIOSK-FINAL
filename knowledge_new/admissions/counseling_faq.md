#### How many Demand Drafts (DDs) do I need to bring for admission into the Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE) branch?

For Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE), you need to bring 4 DDs but only submit 3 DDs to the University: Rs. 4,50,000, Rs. 50,000, and Rs. 10,000. You may retain the remaining DD of Rs. 50,000 with you. DDs must be in favour of "PES UNIVERSITY GENERAL FUND", payable at Bangalore.

#### How many DDs do I need for B.Tech Computer Science Engineering (CSE) admission?

For Computer Science Engineering (CSE) or Computer Science Engineering (AIML), you must submit all four DDs: Rs. 4,50,000, Rs. 50,000, Rs. 50,000, and Rs. 10,000 to the University.

#### How many DDs do I need for Mechanical Engineering, EEE, or Biotechnology admission?

For Mechanical Engineering, Electrical and Electronic Engineering (EEE), or Biotechnology, you submit only two DDs: Rs. 4,50,000 and Rs. 10,000 to the University. You may retain the other two DDs of Rs. 50,000 and Rs. 50,000 with you.

#### What is the total DD amount I need to arrange for the PESU JEE counseling?

You need to prepare four DDs in total in favour of "PES UNIVERSITY GENERAL FUND", payable at Bangalore:
- Rs. 4,50,000
- Rs. 50,000
- Rs. 50,000
- Rs. 10,000

The DDs that you actually submit depend on the branch you select. DDs for partial amounts will not be accepted and you will not be allowed to participate in the seat selection process.

#### I have secured a seat through the EAC round. Do I still need to participate in the second round counseling?

No, participation in the second round PESU JEE counseling is not mandatory if you have already secured a merit B.Tech seat through the Early Admission Commitment (EAC) round or the PESU JEE first round counseling. However, you may optionally participate if you want to try for a change of campus or specialization using your PESU JEE rank card.

#### I have an EAC seat in ECE. Do I need extra DDs if I come for the second round counseling?

Yes. If you have a current EAC seat or PESU JEE seat in Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE) and want to participate in the second round for upgradation, you must bring 1 additional DD for Rs. 50,000.

#### I have an EAC seat in CSE or AIML. Do I need extra DDs for the second round?

No. If you currently hold a seat in Computer Science Engineering (CSE) or Computer Science Engineering (AIML) through EAC or PESU JEE first round and you wish to participate in the second round counseling, you do not need any additional DDs.

#### I surrendered my EAC seat. Do I need all four DDs again for the second round counseling?

If you surrendered your EAC or PESU JEE first round seat and want to participate again in the second round, you do not need to pay the Rs. 10,000 DD again. You need to bring DDs for Rs. 4,50,000, Rs. 50,000, and Rs. 50,000.

#### I have a Higher Fee Category (HFC) seat. What do I need to bring to the second round counseling?

Candidates with a Higher Fee Category (HFC) seat do not need to bring any DDs. Instead, bring your original fee receipt along with a photocopy. If you have not collected your original fee receipt, collect it from the Office of Admissions at PES University Ring Road Campus before attending the counseling.

#### What documents are required for the PESU JEE second round counseling?

You must bring the following original documents along with 2 sets of self-attested photocopies:
1. PESU JEE Rank Card (very important — print from your dashboard)
2. JEE Main Final Score Card from the NTA portal (with QR code visible)
3. PESU JEE Main Application form (download from dashboard)
4. 10+2 / 2nd PUC Marks Card (original)
5. 10th / SSLC Marks Card (original)
6. Transfer Certificate / College Leaving Certificate (if applicable)
7. Migration Certificate (if from a board other than PU Board)
8. 6 Passport and Stamp Size Photos
9. Aadhaar Card
10. PAN Card copy of parent and candidate

Without original documents, the candidate will not be allowed to participate in the seat selection process.

#### Can a parent attend the PESU JEE counseling on behalf of the candidate?

Yes, but only if the candidate is attending the CET Verification Process on the same day or session. In that case, you must download and use the Authorization Letter, get it attested by PES University in person before the day of the counseling, and submit it with proof of the candidate attending CET verification. Attestation on the day of counseling itself is not permitted.

#### What is the counseling schedule for the PESU JEE second round?

Candidates are scheduled in sessions based on their PESU JEE rank:
- Session 1 (Ranks 1–2000): Report at 7:30 AM
- Session 2 (Ranks 2001–4000): Report at 11:30 AM
- Session 3 (Ranks 4001–6000): Report at 3:30 PM

Candidates with PESU JEE ranks above 6000 are not eligible for the counseling.

#### What is the venue for the PESU JEE second round counseling?

The counseling venue is PES University, Ring Road Campus — 100 Feet Outer Ring Road, Banashankari 3rd Stage, Bangalore 560085. It is mandatory for the candidate to attend in person.

#### How do I request a refund if I cancel my admission?

The Program Course Fee may be eligible for a refund as per the current UGC refund norms. For specific details on how to initiate a refund request, please contact the Office of Admissions at admissions@pes.edu or call 080-10-297-297.

#### If I get a seat through KCET counseling after securing a PESU JEE seat, will I get a refund?

Yes. If you secure a seat through Government KCET counseling at PES University for the same campus and specialization after having already secured a PESU JEE quota seat, the University will issue a refund for the PESU JEE quota seat and you can complete your final admission through the KCET quota.

#### What is the minimum eligibility score to apply to PES University?

An aggregate score of 60% in PCM (Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics) in your Class 12 / 2nd PUC examination is mandatory to be eligible for the B.Tech admissions counseling at PES University.
