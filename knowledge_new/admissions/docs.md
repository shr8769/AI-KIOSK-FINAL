### Documents Required

Documents required for admission to specific programs are indicated below

### B.Tech

#### KCET Quota

– CET Admission Order

– 10+2 Marks Card – Original (if the original is not issued by the college, a copy of 10+2 class marks card signed and sealed by the college Principal to be produced)

– 10th Class Marks Card – Original

– Transfer Certificate /College Leaving Certificate

– Migration Certificate (If other than PU Board)

– Copy of Aadhar Card

– Copy of PAN Card of both the Candidate and Parent (Mandatory, for opening bank account)

– Tuition Fee Receipt – Original (Both CET & College)

– SIX COPIES each of color passport size and stamp size photographs

– For Reservation Candidates – Affidavit, Caste, Income Certificate, Rural, Kannada Medium Certificate, Sports, X Defense, NCC Certificate etc…

– Study Certificate

2 sets of self-attested photocopies of the above-mentioned documents

Foreign National Candidates must submit their valid passport copy and valid visa copy (OCI card in case of an OCI). This is mandatory.

#### PESU Merit JEE Main / PESU Merit KCET Quota

– PESU Merit JEE Mains / PESU Merit KCET Rank Card

– Copy of PESU JEE Main / PESU KCET Application Form

– 10+2 Class Marks Card – Original (if the original is not issued by the college, a copy of 10+2 class marks card signed and sealed by the college Principal to be produced)

– Migration Certificate(If other than PU Board)

– Tuition Fee Receipt – Original (Very Important)

– Cancelled Bank Cheque leaf (Very Important)

– Documents pertaining to the Loyalty Program (Only if applicable. For more information on this, please check  [https://pes.edu/loyalty/](https://pes.edu/loyalty/) )

Foreign National Candidates must submit their valid passport copy and valid visa copy (OCI card in case of an OCI). This is mandatory

#### Management/ HFC / NRI/ PIO/ OCI/ Sponsored seats

– JEE Main / KCET Score Card

– 10 + 2 Marks Card – Original (if the original is not issued by the college, a copy of 10+2 class marks card signed and sealed by the college Principal to be produced)

– Eligibility Certificate From AIU (If the grade system is 10+2. This is applicable only for NRI/ OCI/ PIO candidates)

### MBBS

#### KEA - Govt (G) / Private (P) / NRI (N) / Others (Q) Quota

– 6 passport size and 2 stamp size colour photos (Not older than 2 months) Preferably taken with white background.

– KEA Allotment Order (Signature of the Candidate is mandatory)

– Original Fee paid receipt at KEA (Only provide the college copy of the fee receipt)

– Original KEA document verification acknowledgment

– Copy of NEET-2024 Rank/Score card & Admit card

– Copy of 10th Std. / SSLC Marks Card (Proof of Date of Birth)

– Copy of 12th Std. / 2nd PUC Marks card

– Transfer Certificate (12th standard)

– Conduct Certificate (12th standard)

– Study Certificate (12th standard)

– Copy of AADHAAR card (Candidate)

– Copy of the Father’s / Mother’s PAN card

– Caste Certificate (if applicable)

– Income Certificate (if applicable)

– Migration Certificate (for candidates other than Karnataka)

– Eligibility Certificate from RGUHS (If other than Karnataka PU Board)

– Fitness certificate of the candidate issued by a registered medical practitioner

– Copy of General affidavit (Annexure-1) and Rural service bond (Annexure-9) as submitted in KEA

– Discontinuation bond in original to be submitted at the Institution. Template for this can be downloaded from below.

– Authorization of local guardian by parent/guardian (For Hostel in mates). Template for this can be downloaded from below.

– Anti-ragging undertaking by student. Template for this can be downloaded from below.

– Anti-ragging undertaking by parent/guardian. Template for this can be downloaded from below.

– Risk certificate / Indemnity bond. Template for this can be downloaded from below.

– Two sets of self-attested photocopies of the above documents are mandatory
