### Documents Required

Documents required for admission to specific programs are indicated below

### B.Tech

#### KCET Quota

#### PESU Merit JEE Main / PESU Merit KCET Quota

#### Management/ HFC / NRI/ PIO/ OCI/ Sponsored seats

– JEE Main / KCET Score Card– 10 + 2 Marks Card – Original (if the original is not issued by the college, a copy of 10+2 class marks card signed and sealed by the college Principal to be produced)– 10th Class Marks Card – Original– Transfer Certificate /College Leaving Certificate– Migration Certificate(If other than PU Board)– SIX COPIES each of color passport size and stamp size photographs– Copy of Aadhar Card– Copy of PAN Card of both the Candidate and Parent (Mandatory, for opening bank account)– Tuition Fee Receipt – Original (Very Important)– Eligibility Certificate From AIU (If the grade system is 10+2. This is applicable only for NRI/ OCI/ PIO candidates)– Cancelled Bank Cheque leaf (Very Important)– Documents pertaining to the Loyalty Program (Only if applicable. For more information on this, please checkhttps://pes.edu/loyalty/)

2 sets of self-attested photocopies of the above-mentioned documents

Foreign National Candidates must submit their valid passport copy and valid visa copy (OCI card in case of an OCI). This is mandatory.
