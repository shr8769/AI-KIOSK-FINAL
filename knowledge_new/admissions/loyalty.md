### Loyalty Program

Honouring Commitment, Strengthening Bonds!

At PES, we deeply value the trust and dedication of our employees, students, alumni, and their families. As a token of our appreciation,PES Loyalty Programhas been introduced, designed to provide annual fee concessions to those who have been a part of the PES family, and to ensure that the PES legacy continuous through generations.

### Loyalty Program for Current PES Group* Employees

Benefit: 30% fee concession annually for all years of study.

Eligibility:

- Children of full-time current employees on the payroll of PES Group*.
- The parent must have been employed at PES Group* continuously for at least 5 years at the time of the candidate’s admission.

Applicable Programs:

- All* programs of study at PES Group*

### Loyalty Program for Students Graduated (Alumni) from PES Group*

Benefit: 20% fee concession annually for all years of study.

Eligibility:

- Students graduated (alumni) from PES Group*.
- For students of PES Public School Chittoor or SSM School– Students must have studied for atleast 4 years in these respective schools and must have passed 10th Grade from these schools.
- For students of The Amaatra Academy– Students must have studied grade 9, 10, 11 and 12 at The Amaatra Academy to get the aforementioned 20% fee concession.If the student has studied only grade 11 and 12 at The Amaatra Academy, such students will get only 10% fee concession.Students who have studied grade 9 and 10 at The Amaatra Academy will receive 20% fee concession for grade 11 and 12 at The Amaatra Academy.Students who join The Amaatra Academy after studying grade 9 and 10 at PES Public School Chittoor / SSM School will get 10% fee concession for grade 11 and 12 at The Amaatra Academy.
- For students of PES PU College– Students who have studied both 1st PU and 2nd PU at the PES PU College will get this 20% fee concession.Students who join the PES PU College after studying grade 9 and 10 from PES Public School Chittoor / SSM School / The Amaatra Academy will get 10% fee concession for 1st PU and 2nd PU at the PES PU College.
- Students from any other PES Institution must have studied for 3 years in the respective PES Institution to get this benefit.

Applicable Programs:

- All* programs of study at PES Group*

Students who are currently studying at PES Group* and willing to secure admissions in future for a UG / PG program at PES Group* will also be eligible for this benefit.

### Loyalty Program for Siblings of Current Students & Alumni of PES Group*

Benefit: 10% fee concession annually for all years of study.

Eligibility:

- Siblings of Current PES Group* Students & PES Group* Alumni.
- The Current PES Group* Student or the PES Group* Alumni must have studied and completed a minimum of 3 years at the PES Group* for the siblings to get this benefit.

Applicable Programs:

- All* programs of study at PES Group*

### Loyalty Program for Children of PES Group* Alumni

Benefit: 10% fee concession annually for all years of study.

Eligibility:

- Children of PES Group* Alumni.
- The PES Group* Alumni must have studied at PES Group* for a minimum of 3 Years for their children to get this benefit

Applicable Programs:

- All* programs of study at PES Group*

### Important Notes

PES Group*

PES Group*, wherever mentioned on this Loyalty page and in the Loyalty application form refers to PES University (Ring Road Campus, Electronic City Campus, Hanumanth Nagar Campus), PESIMSR – Kuppam, The Amaatra Academy, PES Public School – Chittoor and SSM School.

Applicable for admissions starting from AY 2025-26

This Loyalty Program will be applicable starting from the admissions of AY 2025-26.

Exclusive Benefits

TheLoyalty Program benefits cannot be combined. A student can claim onlyoneloyalty benefit at the time of admission.

Additional Scholarships / Benefits Still Applicable

The loyalty benefit isindependent of other applicable scholarships(e.g.,Prof. MRD Scholarships, Prof. CNR Rao Scholarships etc…) and other applicable benefits likeStudent Assistantships, Teaching Assistantships, and Research Assistantships etc.Meaning, the student will still get all these benefits that they are eligible for in addition to the loyalty benefit.

Not Applicable for Government Quota Admissions

The loyalty benefitdoes not applyto students admitted throughGovernment CET or Government PGCET quota or other Government quota’s.

Disclaimer

- This Loyalty Program is not applicable for MD and MS programs.
- This Loyalty Program has no bearing on the admissions process of any PES Group* Institution. This benefit will only be applicable for eligible candidates if they clear the admissions process of a particular PES Institution as per norms.
- The Loyalty Program mentioned here will be reviewed by PES on a regular basis and may be changed / annulled without any prior notification.
- If a PES Group* full-time employee on the payroll of the PES Group* availing the Loyalty benefit, resigns / relieved from the PES Group*, the Loyalty benefit will be declared null and void.
- PES reserves the right to approve the Loyalty Benefit at its discretion, and its granting is subject to the approval by the Management of PES.

### Building a Stronger PES Community

With this initiative, PES reaffirms its commitment to fosteringacademic excellence and lifelong connections. We take immense pride in our students, employees, and alumni, and we strive to make quality education more accessible for the PES family.

### How to Apply?

If you are selected in the admissions process of a PES Group* Institution and if you are invited to pay the tuition fee and if you are applicable for any of the loyalty benefit mentioned on this page, you can get the Demand Draft (DD) for the applicable tuition fee minus the concession amount you are applicable for. For example, if you are required to pay Rs. 1,00,000 as tuition fee and if you are applicable for 20% concession as per the Loyalty Program, instead of Rs. 1,00,000, you can get a DD for Rs. 80,000.

In addition to the above, go through the application form by clickinghere, download the same, fill it up and submit it along with the DD’s and requisite documents as mentioned above.

This Loyalty Benefit to the eligible candidates is extended in the form of a fee concession at the time of final admissions process, based on the initial claims and documents submitted by the candidate. All claims and documents will be subject to detailed verification and scrutiny at a later stage. In the event of any discrepancy or if the submitted information is found to be inaccurate or not meeting the eligibility criteria for the PES loyalty program, the candidate will be liable to repay the fee concession amount to PES. Failure to do so will result in appropriate action being initiated by the PES.
