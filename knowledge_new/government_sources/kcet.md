# KCET Guidelines and Ingestion Support

The Karnataka Common Entrance Test (KCET) is conducted by the Karnataka Examinations Authority (KEA) for admission to professional courses including Engineering, Technology, Architecture, and others in Karnataka.

## PES University College Codes
- **Ring Road Campus**: College Code `E005`
- **Electronic City Campus**: College Code `E162`

## Eligibility Criteria
- The candidate must be a citizen of India.
- Must have studied and passed in one or more Government or Government recognized educational institutions located in the State of Karnataka for a minimum period of SEVEN academic years.
- Must have passed Qualifying Examination (10+2 / 2nd PUC) with Physics and Mathematics as compulsory subjects along with Chemistry / Bio-Technology / Biology / Electronics / Computer Science.

## KCET Admission & Counseling Process
1. Document Verification: Candidates must upload/submit required documents for verification.
1. Verification Slip: KEA issues a Verification Slip containing the key to log in for option entry.
1. Option Entry: Candidates log in and enter their college preferences (using codes E005 or E162 for PES).
1. Mock Allotment: KEA publishes mock results to let candidates modify their options.
1. Seat Allotment (Round 1 & 2): Seats are allotted based on KCET ranks and college options.
1. Fee Payment & Admission: Allotted candidates pay the fee and report to PES University.
