# COMEDK Guidelines and Ingestion Support

COMEDK UGET is a state-level entrance exam conducted for admission to undergraduate engineering courses in member institutions of the Consortium of Medical, Engineering, and Dental Colleges of Karnataka.

PES University accepts COMEDK ranks for admission to its undergraduate engineering programs (B.Tech) at both Ring Road and Electronic City campuses.

## Eligibility Criteria
- Candidates from all over India are eligible to apply.
- Must have passed 2nd PUC or 12th Std or equivalent exam with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics.
- Must have secured a minimum of 45% marks in Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics combined (40% for SC, ST, and OBC candidates of Karnataka State).

## Counseling Process
1. Online Registration & Upload of Documents for verification.
1. Choice Filling: Candidates choose colleges and courses in order of preference.
1. Seat Allotment: Seat allocation is based on COMEDK rank and choice list.
1. Decision Making: Candidates can Freeze, Float, or Reject allotted seats.
1. Reporting: Successful candidates report to PES University with allotment letter.

## Fees and Tuition Structure
The tuition fee is determined by the Government of Karnataka and COMEDK guidelines annually (typically around Rs. 2,60,000 to Rs. 2,80,000 per year for COMEDK quota, excluding university/other fees).
