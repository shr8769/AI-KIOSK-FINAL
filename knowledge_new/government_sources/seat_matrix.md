## Reservation Policies
- Karnataka Quota (KCET): Reservation as per Government of Karnataka rules including SC (15%), ST (3%), OBC Categories (Category-1 4%, 2A 15%, 2B 4%, 3A 4%, 3B 5%), Rural candidate quota (15%), Kannada Medium quota (5%), and Hyderabad-Karnataka (Article 371J) quota.
- PESSAT Quota: Merit-based admission through university entrance exam, no reservation applies.
- COMEDK Quota: National-level open merit-based counseling, no state reservation applies.
