Physical EducationPESU RR Campus

PES University continues to uphold its commitment to holistic student development, with its yoga athletes delivering an outstanding performance at the 6th Karnataka State Open Yogasana Championship – 2026. The championship was held on July 5,  2026 and organized by the Acharya Yoga Youth Club, R.R. Nagar, Bengaluru, bringing together talented yogasana practitioners from across the state.

The event provided a platform for participants to demonstrate their strength, flexibility, balance, concentration, and mastery of yogasana techniques. Representing PES University and its institutions, students competed with determination and showcased exceptional talent across different age categories.

Remarkable AchievementsThe PES contingent secured impressive results, highlighting the institution's growing excellence in yoga and wellness-based sports.

First PlaceSahana (B.Tech, PES Electronic City Campus) achieved First Place in the Women (20–25 Years Category). Her exceptional performance, precision, and confidence earned her the top position in a highly competitive category.

Second PlaceHarikartik (B.Tech, PES RR Campus) secured Second Place in the Men (20–25 Years Category), demonstrating remarkable skill, discipline, and dedication throughout the competition.

Third PlaceDeepak (PU, PES HN Campus) claimed Third Place in the Men (15–20 Years Category) with an impressive display of yogasana techniques and composure.

Fifth PlaceShashank (PU, PES HN Campus) finished in Fifth Place in the Men (15–20 Years Category), reflecting his hard work and commitment to excellence.

Best Performer RecognitionAdding to the celebrations, Shaarvari V. C. (PES University RR Campus, Bengaluru) was honored with the prestigious A+ Grade Best Performer Award, recognizing her outstanding overall performance and excellence in the championship.

A Testament to Dedication and ExcellenceThese achievements are a reflection of the students’ rigorous training, perseverance, and passion for yoga. Their success also underscores the strong support system provided by PES University, which encourages students to excel not only in academics but also in sports, wellness, and extracurricular pursuits.

Yoga is more than a physical discipline—it fosters mental resilience, focus, and overall well-being. The accomplishments of these students demonstrate how dedication to the practice can translate into excellence on competitive platforms.

Celebrating PES PrideThe PES University community takes immense pride in these exceptional achievements. The university congratulates Sahana, Harikartik, Deepak, Shashank, and Shaarvari V. C. for their remarkable performances and for bringing laurels to the institution.

Their success serves as an inspiration to fellow students and reinforces the importance of discipline, perseverance, and holistic development. PES University wishes them continued success in their future competitions and endeavors.

Congratulations to all the achievers for making PES proud!
