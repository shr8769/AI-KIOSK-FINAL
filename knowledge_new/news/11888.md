Management StudiesPESU RR Campus

The event encouraged students to read books and to develop the skill of extracting relevant content from a given text to create study notes and presentation summaries. To enhance student interest and to provide a spirit of competition the study-a-thon was combined with a treasure hunt inside the library where students had to decode clues to find the topics and tasks for self-study. The department of BHEM conducted the learning drive as part of the Managing Services course on August 22, 2025 at the central library RR campus.
