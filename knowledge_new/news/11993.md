PESU RR Campus

On January 3, 2026, PES University hosted its annual Research Conclave, a vibrant platform that brought together scholars, researchers, and students to explore the frontiers of knowledge and innovation. The event featured distinguished speakers from academia, industry, and research organizations, offering deep insights into diverse aspects of research and its societal impact.

Event HighlightsThe conclave began with a warm welcome by Hon’ble Vice Chancellor Dr. J. Suryaprasad, followed by the distribution of incentives to faculty members for their outstanding research contributions. The event was coordinated by Dean of Research Dr. Manikandan, with support from full-time research scholars across campuses.

Key Sessions & Insights

- Physics, Metaphysics, and ConsciousnessSpeaker: Dr. Chiadananda Gowda, Former Vice-Chancellor, Kuvempu UniversityDr. Gowda explored the fundamental nature of reality, introducing concepts like “It” (physical matter), “Bit” (information), “Qubit” (quantum information), and “Chit” (consciousness)—arguing that consciousness synthesizes information and illuminates the universe.
- Research Innovations and Patents: Business DifferentiatorsSpeaker: Dr. T.V. Ravi, Former Senior Patent CounselThis session highlighted the strategic role of patents in transforming research into market-ready innovations, bridging academia and industry for commercial success.
- Transition from Educators to EntrepreneursSpeaker: Dr. Jagadish G., Professor in Aerospace, IIScDr. Jagadish emphasized that innovation thrives on competence, customer focus, and capital, underscoring persistence and a service-oriented mindset as keys to entrepreneurial success.
- Research Grants Ecosystem in Academic ResearchSpeaker: Shri S. Ganesan, Senior Scientist, ISROHe provided practical guidance on securing government and public sector funding for quality research projects.

Why It MattersThe Research Conclave served as a catalyst for intellectual exchange, inspiring participants to pursue impactful research and innovation. It reinforced PES University’s commitment to fostering a culture of inquiry, collaboration, and entrepreneurship.
