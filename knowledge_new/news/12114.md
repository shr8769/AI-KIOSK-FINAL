PESUIMSR

The Department of Pharmacology at PES University Institute of Medical Sciences and Research (PESUIMSR) continues to foster academic excellence, as evidenced by an impressive performance at the State-Level Pharmacology Quiz Competition held at Apollo Medical College, Chittoor.

The competition brought together around 60 teams from medical colleges across the state, creating a challenging environment for participants to demonstrate their knowledge and analytical skills. The quiz extended beyond the conventional undergraduate MBBS curriculum and covered a wide spectrum of advanced topics, including novel drug therapies, monoclonal antibodies, pharmacogenomics, recent advances in clinical trials, and emerging developments in pharmacotherapeutics.

In a remarkable display of academic strength, all five teams representing PESUIMSR successfully qualified for the semifinal round. This achievement alone reflects the students' strong conceptual understanding, rigorous preparation, and commitment to continuous learning.

The competition results further highlighted the capabilities of PESUIMSR students

- First Prize – Rishi and Navya
- Third Prize – Rakshitha and Sanjana
- Fourth Prize – Zehrah and Rajalakshmi

Adding to this success, the teams securing the seventh and eighth positions were also from PESUIMSR, demonstrating the institution's depth of talent and consistent academic performance across all participating teams.

Such accomplishments are a reflection of the dedication and perseverance of the students, supported by the guidance and mentorship provided by the faculty of the Department of Pharmacology. Their success underscores the department's focus on cultivating critical thinking, scientific curiosity, and a strong foundation in medical sciences.

Participation in academic competitions of this caliber provides students with valuable opportunities to expand their knowledge, engage with contemporary developments in pharmacology, and apply their learning beyond the classroom. The experience also encourages healthy competition, teamwork, and professional growth—qualities that are essential for future healthcare professionals.

PESUIMSR congratulates all the participating students and faculty members on this outstanding achievement. Their performance not only showcases individual excellence but also reflects the institution's continued commitment to nurturing competent, knowledgeable, and forward-thinking medical professionals.

As PESUIMSR continues to emphasize academic rigor and innovation in medical education, achievements such as these serve as an inspiration for future batches to strive for excellence and contribute meaningfully to the evolving field of healthcare.
