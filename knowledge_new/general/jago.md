### JAGO

PES University · Meet the Mascot

##### Shaping Outlook, Inspiring Growth

Say hello to Jago — PES University’s mascot, and the mindset behind the name. Jago encourages every student to embrace life with enthusiasm, confidence, and purpose, built around four simple values: Joyful, Approachable, Generous, and Optimistic.

##### Joyful

Approaching everyday campus life with genuine enthusiasm and positivity.

##### Approachable

Engaging openly with peers, faculty, and the wider community.

##### Generous

Giving time, empathy, and support freely to others around you.

##### Optimistic

Facing challenges with a confident, forward-looking perspective.

Jago’s Values

## Four letters. One outlook.

At PES University, JAGO is more than a mascot — it represents a mindset that encourages students to embrace life with enthusiasm, confidence, and purpose. Built around the values of being Joyful, Approachable, Generous, and Optimistic, JAGO focuses on personal development beyond the classroom — helping students cultivate the attitudes and interpersonal skills needed to thrive in a dynamic world.

JAGO encourages students to explore new ideas, engage meaningfully with people around them, and approach challenges with a positive outlook.

Through co-curricular activities, campus experiences, leadership opportunities, and community engagement, students develop self-awareness, communication skills, empathy, and resilience.
