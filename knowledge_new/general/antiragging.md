### Anti-Ragging

At PES, we are committed to providing a safe, inclusive, and respectful environment for all students. Ragging in any form is strictly prohibited on campus and in associated facilities.

We adhere to the guidelines laid down by the University Grants Commission (UGC) and the National Medical Commission (NMC) to prevent and address incidents of ragging. Any violation will be dealt with seriously, including disciplinary action and legal consequences.
