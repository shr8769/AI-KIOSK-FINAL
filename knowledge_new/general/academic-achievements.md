##### 12th Grade results

Achievements

We are proud to state that this year we’ve received the 100% results award for our consistent performance in the past three years. In fact the director of the PU board had stated on record, that we are the only institution in the southern district to have won this honourable distinction. In fact, many of our students have gone on to acquire meritorious seats in esteemed universities not only in India but also abroad.

##### 10th Grade Results

We are proud to say that we have successfully help graduate two batches of the tenth graders. apart from achieving cent percent results most of them brought laurels to the school by achieving a 10 CGPA.

##### Olympiads and NTSE Exams

Keen to give the children an opportunity to hone their skills thereby achieving a competitive edge, The Amaatra Academy provides a platform to the young learners in the form of Olympiads and NTSE exams. Students right from grade one to twelve are encouraged to participate. In fact we can proudly state that, many of our students have won laurels bringing honour and glory to the school.
