### B.Tech Seat Surrender

For Students who wish to surrender their B.Tech PESU JEE / EAC / B.Tech PESU KCET Seat

### Step 1

Download the Surrender a Seat Form

[Download PDF](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1lKgnXq7ZmRtSfRyF1fcmf-fDrBeanLSz/view?usp=sharing)

### Step 2

Submit it along with the mentioned documents to PES University either in person or through email toadmissions@pes.edu(mentioned documents must be scanned and sent along with the surrender form) or through courier.

If sent through courier, it should be addressed to:Mr. Deepak AnandManager – AdmissionsPES University100 Feet Ring Road, Banashankari 3rd StageBengaluru 560085

### Note

Please check the linkhttps://pes.edu/important-dates/to know the last date of surrender
