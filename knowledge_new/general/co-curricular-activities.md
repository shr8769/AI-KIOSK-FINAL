- Year round, students are trained to excel in dramatics, music, dance, puppetry and art and craft. These painstakingly developed skills are then showcased in an annual extravaganza.
- Our sojourn with studies, in interspaced with well planned intra-school competitions. These draw out the creativity in children and also prepare them for the inter-school competitions that dot the academic year. We not only encourage participation in inter-school events related to music, dance, elocution, drawing and painting etc but also host the most awaited ‘Inter-school Quiz and Debate Competition. This year over eighteen schools participated from all over the region. Though our own children to participate in this event, they are never considered, for the coveted cash awards.
