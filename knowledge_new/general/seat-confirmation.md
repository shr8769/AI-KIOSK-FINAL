### B.Tech Confirmation Process

Candidates who have secured a seat through the PESU Merit EAC Round / PESU Merit JEE Counseling are required to submit theirhard copy original documentsas per the linkhttps://pes.edu/docs/to the Office of Admissions at PES University Ring Road Campus to confirm their existing provisional admission. This needs to be done on or before17th July 2026.

Please note that it is preferable to submit all the original hard copy documents in-person to the University and collect the acknowledgement for the same. However, if you want to send it through courier, you may do so by sending it to…

Mr. Deepak AnandManager – Admissions,PES University,100 Feet Ring Road, Banashankari 3rd Stage, Bengaluru 560085.

- Online Counseling Candidates who took a PESU JEE seat in the counseling can collect their original hard copy fee receipt from the University in person when they come to submit original documents as given above. If such candidates send their original documents through courier, their fee receipt will be with the University and can be collected when they visit the University in person for the Orientation program.
- Candidates who changed from Early Admission Commitment (EAC) seat or Higher Fee Category (HFC) seat to PESU JEE seat in the counseling through online counseling can collect their change of quota receipt in-lieu of the fee receipt from the University when they come to submit the original documents as given above. If such candidates send their original documents through courier, their change of quota receipt (in-lieu of the fee receipt) will be with the University and can be collected when they visit the University in person for the Orientation program.

### Note

Please note that the last date for submission of original documents to the University is 17th July, 2026.

If we do not receive all the original hard copy documents as per the above link to the University by the aforementioned date, the merit seat secured by the candidate will be forfeited by the University, and it will be the responsibility of the candidate to apply and claim the refund for the seat.

Candidates may keep a tab on the important dates page on our website athttps://pes.edu/admissions/important-dates/for any changes in the last date as shared above.
