### International Internships

Global exposure, limitless learning — shaping leaders through international internships.

PES University actively supports students in exploring global career opportunities through international internships.

Specialized sessions, such as those conducted by industry experts like Palash from PAX, provide insights into the application process, areas of specialization, and financial planning for internships abroad.

[Counseling on International Internships](https://www.news.pes.edu/11000/)

These initiatives empower students—especially from programs like BBA HEM—to gain international exposure and enhance their professional readiness in countries such as the USA, France, and Mauritius.
