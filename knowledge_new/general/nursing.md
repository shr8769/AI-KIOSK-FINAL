#### Programme Highlights & Opportunities

- B.Sc in nursing at PESU is one of the most
                                popular nursing programme in India that trains aspirants to practice
                                nursing in different health care settings nationally as well as
                                globally.
- B.Sc in nursing at PESU is a 4-year
                                undergraduate programme where students learn the science and principles
                                of nursing and it imparts theoretical knowledge and clinical exposure to
                                mould qualified nurses as well acquire critical thinking skills,
                                abilities and skills required for a nursing professional.
- Apart from theory classes, students also
                                get practical training in hospitals, laboratories or communities.
- The degree is widely accepted overseas with
                                high level of recognition and admiration in the healthcare industry as
                                well as among the patients and people
- It opens up a lot of job opportunities in
                                the healthcare sector across the globe
- Students could secure high paying jobs in
                                various health sectors worldwide
- B.Sc nursing candidates can also pursue
                                higher studies in MSc Nursing, P.hD nursing and can also choose a
                                specialisation of their interests
