##### Development Of Community Dialysis Machine For Rural India (Funded By Dst)

CORI has developed dialysis machine for rural India. The project is funded by DST and the development is carried out in collaboration with medical experts. The development machine can be deployed in rural India making the cost of dialysis affordable and its services accessible to general public.
