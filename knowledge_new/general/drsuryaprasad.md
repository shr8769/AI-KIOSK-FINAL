### Dr. Surya Prasad J

Vice Chancellor, PES University

### Hear from our Vice Chancellor

PES University vision is “To create Professionally Superior ethically strong global workforce” with the core values being Perseverance, Excellence & Service. The strength of the University lies not only in our commitment to academic excellence but also in its dedication to research, inclusivity and collaboration. As leaders, educators, and learners, we are tasked with pushing the boundaries of knowledge while embracing diverse perspectives that enrich our collective experience.

Every initiative at the University is aimed at enhancing both our academic offerings and our student support systems. These include the development of new interdisciplinary programs, expanded research opportunities, and additional resources for faculty professional development. University encourages all stakeholders to engage actively, share insights, and work collaboratively to shape these efforts.

At the heart of every initiative in the university is the goal of student success is very important. Our faculty and staff continue to foster a conducive environment where students feel challenged, supported, and inspired to achieve their full potential. To our students: Come with your aspirations and be inspired to embark on a journey of success, at PES University we encourage you to take ownership of your learning journey, seek out new experiences, and push the limits of your curiosity.
