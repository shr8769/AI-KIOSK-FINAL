The M.Sc. Psychology program provides an advanced understanding of psychological principles and
					research methods, preparing students for professional practice and academic research. This program

					clinical, forensic, Industrial, and counselling psychology.

#### Why Psychology @ PES University?

PES University offers a meticulously designed M.Sc. Psychology program that integrates both
					traditional and modern psychological theories and practices. This balanced approach ensures students
					are well-versed in the fundamental aspects of psychology while also staying updated with the latest
					trends and research in the field.

A distinctive feature of the M.Sc. Psychology program is the inclusion of hospital-based training,
					where students are exposed to real-world clinical settings. This not only allows them to apply their
					theoretical knowledge but also helps them acclimatize to working with patients from diverse
					backgrounds.

## CareerOutcomes

The M.Sc. Psychology program at PES University prepares graduates for diverse career paths
							across various psychological fields by offering a strong foundation and specializations in
							Clinical, Forensic, Counselling, and Industrial Psychology. The program provides extensive
							training and practical experience, making graduates well-suited for careers in Business,
							Education, Law, Medicine, Social Work, and specialized areas within Psychology. Potential
							career outcomes include:

#### Clinical Psychologist

Working in mental health clinics, hospitals, or private practice, diagnosing and
										treating mental, emotional, and behavioral disorders.

#### Forensic Psychologist

Collaborating with law enforcement, legal professionals, and
										correctional facilities to assess, counsel, and provide expert testimony on
										psychological issues in legal cases.

#### Counselling Psychologist

Assisting individuals, couples, and families in managing life
										challenges, mental health, and emotional well-being through various therapeutic
										techniques.

#### Industrial and Organizational Psychologist

Supporting businesses with workforce development, employee
										satisfaction, and productivity, applying psychological principles to
										organizational settings.

#### Career and Marriage Counsellor

Providing guidance on career development or relationship issues,
										often in private practice, educational institutions, or social service agencies.
