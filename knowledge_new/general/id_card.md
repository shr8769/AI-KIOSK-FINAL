# ID Card Replacement Procedure

Your PES University ID Card is an essential document that you must carry at all times while on campus. 

## Lost ID Card
If a student has lost their ID card, they must follow these strict procedures:
1. **File an FIR:** The student must immediately file an FIR (First Information Report) with the local police station regarding the lost ID card.
2. **Approach Admissions Office:** After obtaining a copy of the FIR, the student must bring it to the Admissions Office.
3. The Admissions Office will verify the FIR and issue a replacement ID card. A replacement fee may apply.

## Damaged ID Card
If your ID card is damaged due to normal wear and tear or physical bending:
- Take your damaged card to the Campus Card Office or Admissions Office.
- If the damage is due to normal wear and tear, it may be replaced. If it is due to misuse or physical bending, a replacement fee applies.
