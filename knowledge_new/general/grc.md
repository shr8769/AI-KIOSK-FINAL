### Grievances Redressal Comittee (GRC)

About PES University Grievances Redressal Committee: The PES University established a Grievances Redressal Committee (GRC) as per the notification of UGC Grievances Redressal Regulation, 2012 – dated 7th December 2018 (F.No.14-4/2012(CPP-II). This committee aimed at addressing and effectively resolving grievances of the students related to HEI

### Objective of GRC

- To encourage the Students to express their grievances / problems freely and frankly, without any fear of being victimized.
- To set up a mechanism for speedy and expeditious resolution of the grievance.
- To provide appropriate counseling to the students in the process of resolving the grievance.
- To promote cordial relationships amongst the students.

### Functions of GRC

- To review the complaints received by the GRC.
- To follow the principles of natural justice in considering the grievances.
- To report with recommendations, if any, to the Vice Chancellor
- To conduct the surveys to identify the problems of students and provide suitable solutions.

#### PESU GRC MEETING # 06

Minutes of Anti Ragging Committee(GRC) Meeting

Meeting Code: PESU/GRC/2021-22 -#2:

The GRC meeting #6 was convened on 26-05-2022 at the Office of Dean Student Affairs 2.00 pm to 3.00 pm to discuss the following agenda points:

- Action Taken Report of the suggestions made in previous meeting dated: 09-12-2021
- Student Hostel visit by the Committee members
- Any Student Grievance
- Any other suggestions

The meeting started with the Chairman discussing the action taken as per the suggestions given by the committee members on 09-12-2021. Subsequently the Chairman opened the discussion on the agenda points.

- The Student Counselor continued in bringing the student responses to the attention of the Vice Chancellor / Registrar and the Grievance Committee.
- The Chairman and the committee members visited the hostel and observed that good hygiene practices are maintained in the hostel. The committee suggested the supervisor of the hostel canteen to maintain a healthy and nutritious food for the students.
- The IT infrastructure including the WiFi facility in the campus was upgraded.
- A new facility called PES 52 was created at PES University enabling the students to organize and participate in Hackathons.
- The chairman and the committee members decided to prepare a schedule and visit the department to interact with the students and staff members.
- The committee decided to recommend upgrading the library facilities of PES University to accommodate a larger number of students and staff.
- The Chairman suggested all members to propose a plan for a workshop on creating awareness to the staff and students of PES University regarding the limitation or preference having adverse effect on equality of treatment in education or in employment in depriving a student or a group of students or employees on the basis of caste, creed, religion, language, ethnicity, gender, disability.

The meeting concluded by Convener, thanking all the members.
