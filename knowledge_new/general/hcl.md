### HCLTech and PES University: Empowering Workforce with WILP

PES University is happy to partner with HCLTech, extending academic upskilling through Work Integrated Learning Program. HCLTech employees are encouraged to enrol in our various programs in Engineering. This partnership empowers HCLTech employees to tap into the most advanced technologically evolved resources and professional faculty expertise in PES University, supporting individual academic aspirations for professional growth.

### Unique Features of Programs at PESU

PES is among the country’s leading teaching and research universities, with more than a 50 year history of innovation and all-round student development.

PES Vision “To create professional superior and ethically strong global workforce” along with core values of “Perseverance, Excellence & Service” have remained unchanged and reflects our holistic approach to education.

PES has a robust and tangible metric based set of KPI “SPIRIT – Student, Process, Identity, Research, Infrastructure & Teacher” which sets the roles of stakeholders for quality education.
