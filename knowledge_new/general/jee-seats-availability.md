### PESU JEE Round 2 Seats Availability

The 2nd round of PESU JEE Counseling will be happening on 25th July 2026.

With regards to this, we would like to bring to your notice that we have had an overwhelming response for our PESU JEE round 1 counseling and the tentative number of seats that might be available for this second round PESU JEE counseling is as follows…

### Ring Road Campus

| Branch | Available Seats |
| --- | --- |
| CSE | 41 |
| CSE(AIML) | 26 |
| ECE | 4 |
| EEE | 1 |
| Mechanical | -1 (minus 1) |
| Biotechnology | 10 |

### Electronic City Campus

| Branch | Available Seats |
| --- | --- |
| CSE | 42 |
| CSE(AIML) | 31 |
| ECE | 6 |

### Note

Please note, we are over-subscribed in mechanical as you see above, however, this over-subscription might even-out during the seat selection process. Please be aware of the availability of the seats as you can see above and we would like to re-emphasize that although we would like to satisfy the expectations of every student, we are however bound by the reality of the available seats and in the light of this, we would request you to manage your expectations based on this data and attend the counseling with realistic expectations.

All other prior information shared regarding the counseling process will hold good.
