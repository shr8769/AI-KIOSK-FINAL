## About the Program

The CIE Industry Mentor Program connects PES University students with accomplished professionals from leading technology companies, startups, and organizations. Our mentors bring decades of combined experience across diverse domains including software engineering, product management, entrepreneurship, AI/ML, semiconductors, and more.

### Industry Expertise

Connect with seasoned professionals from top companies like Google, Microsoft, Intel, Wipro, and more

### Personalized Guidance

Receive one-on-one mentorship tailored to your career goals and interests

### Networking Opportunities

Build valuable connections with industry leaders and expand your professional network

### Skill Development

Gain practical insights and develop skills that matter in today's competitive job market

### Real-world Insights

Learn about current industry trends, technologies, and best practices from practitioners

### Career Acceleration

Fast-track your career with guidance on job search, interviews, and career decisions
