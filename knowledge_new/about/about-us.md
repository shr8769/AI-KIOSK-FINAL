### CORI

The team at CORI is a mix of diverse and experienced talent. This diversity is what makes CORI a unique resource. Since its formation in 2010 CORI has undertaken an impressive range of projects demanding multidisciplinary research and innovative solutions. Many organizations which are known for their cutting edge research capabilities and pioneering work have entrusted CORI with these projects.

The engineers and scientists bring to the research efforts the experience of handling complex and large projects and programs for the government and industry. CORI is excited by the journey from creating an idea, forming a working concept, building a prototype, and solving the problem. Partnering in this journey are industries and research organizations.
