### History

### PES was born as the People’s Education Society in 1973, to provide education to underprivileged children. We have grown into one of India’s leading universities, but our values of Perseverance, Excellence and Service, remain unchanged.

### 2020s

Recognized as one of India’s leading universities with a strong focus on Perseverance, Excellence, and Service.

### 2013–2019

Rapid growth in academic programs, research, infrastructure, and digital learning platforms like PESU Academy.

### 2013

PES Institute of Technology (PESIT) gains university status and becomes PES University.

### 2000s

Establishment of engineering and management institutions under PES.

### 1972–1980s

Expansion into pre-university and undergraduate education.

### 1965

Founded as the People’s Education Society (PES) by Prof. M.R. Doreswamy, starting with a school for underprivileged children in Thyagarajanagar, Bengaluru.
