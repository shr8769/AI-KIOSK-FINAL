The Centre for Innovation and Entrepreneurship (CIE) at PES University offers industry partners a dynamic platform to engage with our innovation ecosystem through webinars, networking events, and industry-specific workshops.

Discover the unique opportunities that CIE events provide for industry partners to connect, collaborate, and drive innovation within our dynamic ecosystem.

### Explore Collaborations

## Event Opportunities for Industry

Choose from various engagement formats to connect with CIE's ecosystem and share your expertise with our vibrant community of students, faculty, and innovators.

### Webinars

Most Popular

Virtual sessions on AI, cybersecurity, or sustainability, featuring industry experts and CIE faculty

#### Format:

Virtual sessions with interactive Q&A

#### Example Success:

"AI for Road Safety" webinar with Dr. Anbumani Subramanian engaged 100+ participants, fostering dialogue on AI applications in transportation

#### Benefits:

- Share expertise with tech-savvy audience
- Network with potential collaborators virtually
- Gain visibility among PES students and faculty
