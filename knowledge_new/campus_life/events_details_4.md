## About this Event

Avyakta – The Living Traditions, the cultural club of PESU ECC, is looking for passionate, creative, and driven students to join our journey.

This is not just a club. It is about creating unforgettable experiences, curating meaningful events, and bringing powerful ideas to life on campus. From vibrant celebrations to something grand and never seen before, exciting days are ahead, and we want you to be a part of it.

We are looking for students who are ready to take responsibility, grow, and make a real impact across our core domains:

LogisticsSocial MediaEvent ManagementEthics & DisciplineFinance & SponsorshipOperationsTechnicalDesignMarketingThe registrations are currently open, and the deadline for submitting applications is  March 9, 2026.
