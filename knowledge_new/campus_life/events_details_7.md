## About this Event

Organized by the Centre for Healthcare Engineering and Learning (cHEAL), PES University, HEAL-A-Thon 2026 is a 10-hour state-level healthcare hackathon that brings together students passionate about healthcare and technology.

The hackathon is designed to foster innovation in digital health, medical technology, and patient-centered solutions. Participants will collaborate in teams to solve real-world healthcare challenges through creative problem-solving, prototyping, and technical implementation.

It provides a platform to transform impactful ideas into practical solutions with guidance from mentors and domain experts.

FormatTeam-based competition (3–4 members per team)10-hour offline hackathonProblem statements released at the start of the eventMentor review rounds during developmentFinal pitch presentation before expert juryEvaluation based on innovation, feasibility, technical implementation, impact, and presentation

Hackathon TracksmHealth – Mobile and digital solutions to improve patient care and accessibilityAI in Healthcare – AI/ML-based diagnostics, prediction, and healthcare analyticsHealthcare Systems & Devices – Smart systems, IoT, and assistive medical technologiesPublic Health & Preventive Care – Community health, monitoring, and awareness solutions

Key DetailsDuration: 10 HoursEligibility: Students interested in Healthcare & Technology (Teams of 3–4 members)

Rewards & BenefitsAttractive PrizesMentor guidance from healthcare & technology expertsNetworking with innovators and domain professionalsCertificate of Participation for all teams
