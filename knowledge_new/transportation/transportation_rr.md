### Procedure to Enroll for PESU – Transportation

1. Please fill and submit the “Online Application Form” with all the details.The OnlineApplication Form will be enabled starting from 3rd July 2026.Note:SRN/PRN is a 13 digit Alpha-Numeric Number.The Login ID in the Student Copy of Admission Acknowledgement is the PRN. Please contact the admissionsdepartment if you do not have it.
2. Once the application details are verified, an E-mail shall be sent to you on the nextworking day with the instruction for the online payment of the Transportation fee.
3. Payment confirmation notification will be sent after payment is verified.
4. The payment receipt (hard copy) will be issued on a particular schedule starting from15th September 2026.

[Online Form](https://support.pes.edu/rrtransportation/)

### Note

1. Enrollment for the transport facility is on first come first serve basis only. No furtherrequests will be entertained in this matter.
2. Bus fee once paid will not be refunded/adjusted under any circumstances from 1stSeptember 2026. However, during the month of August, fee refund is available with a10% deduction as processing fee.
3. For the convenience of students and staff,PES University runs a shuttle bus service (free of cost) to the Mysore Road Metro Station..
