### Procedure to Enroll for PESU – Transportation

1. Please fill and submit the “Online Application Form” with all the details. **The Online**
****Application Form will be enabled starting from 3rd July 2026.********Note: SRN/PRN is a 13 digit Alpha-Numeric Number. The Login ID in the Student Copy of Admission Acknowledgement is the PRN. Please contact the admissions
department if you do not have it.****
2. Once the application details are verified, an E-mail shall be sent to you on the next
working day with the instruction for the online payment of the Transportation fee.
3. Payment confirmation notification will be sent after payment is verified.
4. The payment receipt (hard copy) will be issued on a particular schedule starting from
15th September 2026.

[Online Form](https://support.pes.edu/rrtransportation/)

### Note

1. Enrollment for the transport facility is on first come first serve basis only. No further
requests will be entertained in this matter.
2. Bus fee once paid will not be refunded/adjusted under any circumstances from 1st
September 2026. However, during the month of August, fee refund is available with a
10% deduction as processing fee.
3. For the convenience of students and staff, **PES University runs a shuttle bus service (free of cost) to the Mysore Road Metro Station.**. Timings of the pickup and drop are as below…

1. Morning:

- Pick up at 7:30 AM from the Metro Station for 8.15 AM Batch
- Pick up at 7:40 AM from the Metro Station for 8.15 AM Batch
- Pick up at 8:15 AM from the Metro Station for 8.45 AM Batch
2. Evening:

- Drop at 3:45 PM to the Metro Station
- Drop at 5:00 PM to the Metro Station
3. In case of any queries, contact
Mr. Guruprasad (Transport In-Charge).
Email: transport.pesurrcampus@pes.edu.
Mobile: 9113804054.
4. Payments can be made Online and by DD in favour of ‘PES University General Fund’ payable at Bangalore.
5. Cash/Cheque Not Accepted.
