### Procedure to Enroll for PESU – Transportation

1. Please fill and submit the “Online Application Form” with all the details. We are accepting the Online Application Form starting from 17TH July 2026.
2. Once the application details are verified, an E-mail with the “Payment link” for online payment shall be sent.
3. Notification will be sent after payment verification.
4. The payment receipt (hard copy) will be issued on a particular schedule starting from August 2026.

[Online Form](https://support.pes.edu/ectransportation)

### Note

1. Please be informed that even if your classes get over early, all route buses will depart at 5:00 PM sharp from the EC Campus in the evening.
2. Bus fee once paid will not be refunded.
3. Please note that BMTC buses will operate on Route No. 1 and Route No. 5.
4. Email: In case of any queries, contactMr. Shivaraj (Transport Manager) ontransport.pesueccampus@pes.edu
