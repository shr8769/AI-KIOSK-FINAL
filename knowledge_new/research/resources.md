# Resources @ PES

A centralized hub of tools, platforms, and support systems that empower PES University's research community to work efficiently, collaborate easily, and achieve impactful academic outcomes.

The Research Resources section at PES University is designed to give scholars, faculty, and research groups a centralized space to access the tools, platforms, and support systems that enable impactful academic work.

## Unified Research Portal

At the core of our ecosystem is the Research Portal — a single platform that brings together essential research-related information and services. It connects PhD scholars, research centres, and academic event listings in one integrated interface, enabling smooth navigation through the various stages of the research journey. Whether you need to access policies, track documentation, or stay updated on academic milestones, the portal provides a streamlined and reliable environment to support your work.

## Campus-Wide Research Support

Researchers across PES benefit from coordinated support available on both the RR Campus and the EC Campus, including guidance from research administrators, access to seminar and event updates, and connectivity to research initiatives across departments. Quick links to research domains, centres, innovation programs, and contact information ensure that every scholar has direct access to the knowledge networks and infrastructure required to advance their ideas.

## Information Access & Scholar Services

The resources hub also offers consolidated access to vital information such as research policies, funding pathways, publication support, and patent-related details. These services help scholars work within a clear, supportive framework while ensuring that research adheres to academic standards and institutional guidelines. By centralizing these touchpoints, PES University empowers its researchers to focus on discovery, problem-solving, and high-quality academic output.
