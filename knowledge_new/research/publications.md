# Publications

The university encourages faculty and students to publish their research findings in reputable journals and conferences worldwide.

PES University supports the publication of research work in high-impact journals and conferences. The university provides resources and guidance to help researchers prepare and submit their work for publication. Faculty members are encouraged to publish their research in peer-reviewed journals, while students are supported in presenting their work at national and international conferences.
