# Innovation @ PES

PES University fosters a dynamic environment for innovation, synergizing education with industry through a unique 360-degree approach.

At PES, we are committed to fostering a culture of innovation and entrepreneurship.

Our two flagship centres,PES Venture Labs (PVL)and theCentre for Innovation and Entrepreneurship (CIE), play a pivotal role in this mission — providing world-class infrastructure, mentorship, and direct industry interfacing to transform ideas into real-world impact.

## Centre for Innovation & Entrepreneurship

Inspiring students to develop path-breaking ideas and helping them become role-model entrepreneurs.

The Centre for Innovation and Entrepreneurship (CIE) serves as a launchpad for student entrepreneurs, providing world-class infrastructure, dedicated faculty and opportunities to interface with industry. CIE is dedicated to inspiring students to develop world-changing ideas.

## PES Venture Labs

Incubating and supporting startups with potential to help them go from concept to market.

PES Venture Labs (PVL) transforms dedicated students into entrepreneurs. PVL has invested in over 24 deep-tech startups across diverse sectors. Initiatives like 'Unicorn Launchpad' identify campus-based ideas with unicorn potential, providing evaluation, funding, and comprehensive support.

[Visit PVL Website](https://www.pesuventurelabs.com/)
