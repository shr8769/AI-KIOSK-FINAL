# Research Policy

The pursuit of quality, the impact of research and the general research environment are achieved through our comprehensive research policy framework.

PES University research policy plan outlines the way in which the university wishes to realize its ambitions in the field of Research. This Research Policy is a guideline for priorities in research, the use of research funds, faculty members research plans and initiatives.

Research at PES University aims to lead high-quality and impactful research academically and socially. PES University continues to invest in a wide range of research areas, in a pleasant working environment and in a fruitful symbiosis with many stakeholders in research.
